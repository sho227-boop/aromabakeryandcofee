Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This is a general-purpose privacy policy template for the Aroma Bakery & Coffee website. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and should be customized and legally reviewed before it is relied upon.

What this site collects

This website is primarily informational. It does not include a contact or order form. Visitors may reach Aroma Bakery & Coffee directly by phone, email or Instagram — links on this site simply hand off to your phone's dialer, email client or the Instagram app, and any information you share through those channels is subject to their own privacy practices, not this site's.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. If analytics or advertising tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and location links open Google Maps and Instagram. These third parties may collect standard technical information (such as IP address) when their resources load, governed by their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Aroma Bakery & Coffee at aromabakerycoffe@gmail.com or via Instagram.
