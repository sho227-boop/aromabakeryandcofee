Our Story

Aroma Bakery & Coffee

Aroma Bakery & Coffee is a Philadelphia bakery and cafe built around housemade desserts, thoughtfully prepared coffee and tea, and cakes made to order. It's a place designed for the everyday — a coffee before work, a slice shared with a friend — as much as for the moments worth celebrating.

Across two Philadelphia locations, Old City and Northeast Philadelphia, the same idea holds: good ingredients, unhurried craft, and a room that feels good to sit in.

What We Make

Desserts, drinks, and cakes made to order.

Mousse cakes, tartlets, European-style pastries and cookies sit alongside espresso, matcha and tea, prepared the same way each time. Custom cakes are built individually for birthdays, showers and celebrations, made when they're ordered rather than pulled from a case.

Made for everyday moments, and the ones worth celebrating.
